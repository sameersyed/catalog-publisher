# Data Notice

This repository contains independently derived, compact financial-data artifacts sourced from public
SEC EDGAR submissions and extracted XBRL Company Facts.

- The SEC remains the authoritative source. Every artifact records source URLs, retrieval timestamps,
  and SHA-256 hashes.
- This project is not affiliated with, approved by, or endorsed by the SEC or any issuer.
- SEC and EDGAR names are used only as textual source references. No SEC seal, EDGAR logo, or other
  agency artwork is used.
- Artifacts contain selected public numerical facts and filing metadata, not complete filings or
  exhibits.
- Artifacts may become stale or be affected by post-acceptance SEC corrections or removals.
- No market-data payloads, personal holdings, workbook identifiers, credentials, or personal Zakat
  calculations are published.
- The data and calculations are provided without warranty and are not financial, tax, legal, or
  religious advice.

Source access follows the SEC fair-access policy: an identified user agent, efficient requests, and a
request rate below the SEC's published maximum.
